# Sales & POS

Tax invoices, collection and cash sessions.

## Invoices
- From a job card or a direct invoice. VAT is calculated per line.
- **Posting** issues the stock and books revenue and cost. A posted invoice cannot be edited; corrections use a credit note.

## Point of sale
1. Open a **cash session** with an opening float.
2. Scan barcodes, choose the customer and the price level.
3. Collect cash, card or a split payment, or hold the sale.

## Closing the session
When closing, the system shows:
- the number of invoices and total sales;
- receipts and refunds per payment method;
- the **expected cash** = opening + cash received − cash refunded.

Enter the counted cash; if there is a difference **a reason is required**, and the shortage or overage is posted automatically.

## Returns
Create a **credit note** from the invoice, returning parts to stock and refunding the amount.
