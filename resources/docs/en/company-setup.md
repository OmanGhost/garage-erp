# Company & branch setup

Set up company details, branches, warehouses, working hours and numbering before going live.

## Company details
In **Settings → Company**: name in Arabic and English, VAT number, commercial registration, logo, currency (Omani rial, three decimals) and VAT rate.

## Branches & warehouses
- Every branch has at least one warehouse.
- Users can be limited to a branch or allowed all branches.

## Working hours & holidays
Used by workshop scheduling, appointments and the **after-hours** control. Enter the hours of each day and public holidays.

## Document numbering
In **Document numbering** set the prefixes (e.g. JC for job cards, INV for invoices) and the sequence that restarts every year.

## Print & notification templates
- Invoices and vouchers print on A4, POS receipts on an 80 mm thermal printer.
- Edit WhatsApp, SMS and email texts under **Notification templates**.
