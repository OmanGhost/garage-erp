# Memberships & loyalty

Recurring income and customers who come back.

## Membership plans
Define the plan: price, duration, included services and quantities, parts and labour discounts and roadside calls.

## Selling a membership
From the customer file choose **Sell** → an invoice is created; the membership becomes active when it is posted.

## Using the membership
On a draft invoice press **Apply membership benefits**: included services become free and discounts apply to the rest. The balance is consumed only on posting.

## Points & tiers
- Points on every posted invoice, silver and gold tiers with a multiplier.
- Redemption as a payment on the invoice.
- Gift cards, coupons, referrals and personal offers for inactive customers.
