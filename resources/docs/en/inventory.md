# Inventory & spare parts

Parts, warehouses, costing and stock counts.

## Parts
- Part number, barcode, brand, category and selling prices (retail, wholesale, workshop).
- **Cross references:** link the OEM number to aftermarket, used and compatible alternatives.
- Reorder level and economic order quantity.

## Costing
Weighted average or FIFO in settings. Every receipt, issue and transfer is posted to accounting automatically.

## Movements
- **Transfers** between warehouses, sent and received.
- **Adjustments** for gains or losses (damage, loss) — logged with reason and user.
- **Stock counts**, periodic or cycle: print the count sheet, enter quantities and post the differences.

## Analytics
ABC analysis, dead stock, stock aging, demand forecast and automatic reorder.
