# Reception & job cards

The full path of a vehicle through the workshop.

## Reception
1. Find the customer by phone or plate, or add them.
2. Record the odometer, fuel level, belongings, body notes and photos.
3. Write the customer's complaint. The job card opens as **Pending inspection**.

## Inspection & quotation
- Record the inspection, then create a **quotation** and send it by link.
- The customer approves all or some items; approved items become tasks and parts.

## Work
- Assign tasks to technicians, who start, pause and finish them; time is tracked automatically.
- Request and issue parts. Extra work after approval needs the customer's consent.
- Work can be sent to an outside specialist from the tasks tab (**Send work outside**).

## Quality & delivery
1. The final quality check moves the job to **Ready for delivery**.
2. Create the invoice from the job card and post it.
3. Collect the payment, hand the vehicle to the owner or an authorised person and print the gate pass.

> A vehicle cannot be delivered without a posted invoice (except warranty, internal and fleet jobs), nor while its part is still with a subcontractor.
