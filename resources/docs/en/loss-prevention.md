# Fraud & loss prevention

How the internal control works and how to review alerts.

## What is watched
High manual discounts, sales below cost, prices below the list, selling-price cuts, cancelled receipts, returns, deleted held sales, stock write-offs, repeated adjustments, issued parts not invoiced at delivery, cash shortages and after-hours work.

## Thresholds
Under **Fraud & loss prevention → Thresholds** set the percentages and amounts of each rule.

## Alerts
- Medium and high alerts reach management at once; low ones come in a daily digest.
- Repeated alerts are grouped into one alert with a counter.

## Review
Open the alert, check the details and the linked record, then choose **Justified** or **Confirmed misconduct** with your notes. Staff cannot review alerts about their own actions.

## Audit log
Every record is digitally sealed and cannot be changed or deleted from the application. Press **Verify audit log** to detect any change made directly in the database.
