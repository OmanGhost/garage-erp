# Getting started

A short guide for your first day: from creating the account to your first invoice.

## 1. Create your account
Request the free trial from **Start trial**. Once the request is verified, your login details arrive by email.

## 2. Complete the company profile
In **Settings** enter the company name, VAT number, logo, address and VAT rate (5 % by default). See [Company setup](company-setup).

## 3. Add users
Add your staff and give each a role: receptionist, technician, storekeeper, cashier, accountant… See [Users & roles](users-roles).

## 4. Enter master data
- **Services:** oil change, inspection… with price and estimated time.
- **Parts:** by hand or imported from a CSV file (saved from Excel), with the opening balance.
- **Customers & vehicles:** created automatically at the first check-in.

## 5. Your first job
1. **Reception:** record the vehicle, odometer and photos.
2. **Job card:** add services and parts and approve it.
3. **Work:** the technician starts the tasks and parts are issued.
4. **Invoice:** create the invoice from the job card and post it.
5. **Delivery:** collect the payment and hand the vehicle over.

> Tip: start with one branch and one warehouse; add branches later.
