# Planning & vehicle tracking

Scheduling technicians and bays, the workshop screen, vehicle and key locations.

## Workshop planner
- **Auto schedule:** approved tasks are spread over available technicians and bays by working hours and leave.
- Drag a task by hand; clashes are refused.
- The forecast completion appears on the job card.

## Live workshop screen
In **Live workshop screen** create a secret link and open it on the workshop TV. It shows jobs, appointments and late work and refreshes itself.

## Vehicle & key tracking
- Define **yard locations** (parking, bays, wash…).
- Move vehicles between locations and record who took the key.
- Print the **key tag** with a QR code and scan it with a camera or RFID reader.

## Road tests
Create a road-test permit with the driver and odometer before leaving, and record the odometer on return. Exceeding the allowed distance raises an alert.
