# Purchasing

From purchase request to supplier invoice.

## The cycle
1. **Purchase request** from a department or the reorder suggestions.
2. **RFQ** to several suppliers and comparison.
3. **Purchase order**, approved and sent to the supplier.
4. **Goods receipt**, full or partial — stock goes up.
5. **Supplier invoice** matched to the receipt and posted to payables.

## Extra costs
Spread freight and customs over the received parts so the cost is real.

## Returns & payments
Return goods with a purchase return and pay invoices with payment vouchers.
