# Accounting

Automatic entries and financial reports.

## Chart of accounts
A ready chart is created for every company. Add sub-accounts as needed; system-linked accounts cannot be deleted.

## Automatic entries
Invoices, receipts, goods receipts, supplier invoices, adjustments and payroll are all posted as balanced entries linked to their documents.

## Manual journals
For special adjustments. A posted entry is never deleted; it is reversed with a reversing entry.

## Closing
Close each month to block back-dated postings, and close the fiscal year at its end.

## Reports
Trial balance, income statement, balance sheet, cash flow, account statements, aging and VAT reports.
