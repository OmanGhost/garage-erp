# Digital vehicle inspection

A photo inspection report the customer approves from their phone.

## Starting an inspection
From the job card choose **New inspection** and a template (full inspection, pre-purchase…).

## Recording results
- For each item: OK / needs attention / urgent, with a measurement when relevant.
- Mark the damage on the **vehicle diagram** and attach photos.
- Suggest the service or part needed for each finding.

## Sending the report
Press **Send to customer**; they receive a link with photos, colours and prices, approve or decline each item and sign.

## After approval
Approved items become a quotation or tasks. Later you can **compare** the inspection before and after the repair.
