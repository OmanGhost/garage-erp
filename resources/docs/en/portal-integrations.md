# Customer portal & integrations

Customer self-service and connecting other systems.

## Customer portal
Enable access from the customer file; customers sign in with their email address to see their cars, jobs and invoices and book appointments.

## Notifications
Connect the SMS, WhatsApp and email providers under **Integrations**, and edit the texts under **Notification templates**.

## API
- Create an **access token** with the abilities you need.
- Send it in the header: `Authorization: Bearer <token>`.
- Use **webhooks** to receive events (invoice posted, job ready…) signed with a secret.

## Online store
Sell parts online with card payment or payment on collection.
